# Muster & Partner Steuerberatung – New GmbH Client Intake SOP

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT.** Muster & Partner Steuerberatung is a fictional firm. This SOP was written for the TaxHub demo. It describes internal workflow preferences only. It is not tax or legal guidance and must not be cited as such.

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines what the intake team collects before a new GmbH client is assigned to an adviser. The goal: an adviser opens the case and can start work without a second round of "please send us…" emails.

A case is **ready for assignment** when every item in section 3 is either received or explicitly marked as "not applicable" with a reason, and every open question in section 4 has an answer or is escalated.

## 2. Case categories

The intake team assigns exactly one primary category and any number of secondary tags.

| Code | Category | Typical trigger |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| NG-01 | New GmbH – company formation support | Company founded recently, no tax registration yet |
| NG-02 | New GmbH – takeover from previous adviser | Existing GmbH switching to us |
| BK | Ongoing bookkeeping (Finanzbuchhaltung) | Client wants us to do or review the books |
| PR | Payroll (Lohnbuchhaltung) | Client has or will have employees |
| JA | Annual financial statements (Jahresabschluss) | Year-end, Bilanz, E-Bilanz |
| AD | Ad-hoc advisory question | One-off question, no ongoing mandate |

A newly founded GmbH asking for bookkeeping and payroll is filed as **NG-01** with tags **BK** and **PR**.

## 3. Required information and documents for a new GmbH (NG-01)

### 3.1 Company master data

1. Exact company name as registered (Firma, incl. "GmbH")
2. Registered office (Sitz) and business address, if different
3. Date of notarisation of the articles of association (Gründungsdatum / Beurkundung)
4. Date of entry in the Handelsregister and HRB number, or status "entry pending"
5. Share capital (Stammkapital) and how much has been paid in
6. Shareholders (Gesellschafter) with shareholdings in %
7. Managing director(s) (Geschäftsführer)
8. Business purpose (Unternehmensgegenstand) in one or two sentences
9. Start of business operations (actual or planned)
10. Financial year (Wirtschaftsjahr) – calendar year or deviating

### 3.2 Tax registration status

11. Has the "Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung" been submitted via ELSTER? If yes: when, and by whom?
12. Tax number (Steuernummer), if already assigned
13. VAT ID (USt-IdNr.), if already assigned or requested
14. Expected turnover in the first and second calendar year (needed for the Fragebogen)
15. Does the client want us to prepare and submit the Fragebogen? (default: yes, via our ELSTER access as authorised representative)

### 3.3 Bookkeeping

16. Bookkeeping software in use (e.g. Lexoffice, sevDesk, DATEV Unternehmen online, none)
17. Who books today: client, us, or nobody yet
18. Business bank account: bank name, whether online banking export is available
19. Preferred document flow (upload to software, DATEV Unternehmen online, email – email is discouraged)

### 3.4 Payroll (only if employees now or within 3 months)

20. Number of employees and planned start dates
21. Type of employment per person: full-time, part-time, Minijob, Werkstudent
22. Gross salary per person
23. Is the managing director also a shareholder? (Social-security status must be checked by an adviser – see section 5)
24. Betriebsnummer from the Bundesagentur für Arbeit (or status "not yet requested")
25. Chosen health insurer(s) for each employee

### 3.5 Previous adviser

26. Name of any previous tax adviser, and whether the mandate has been terminated
27. For NG-02 only: handover of DATEV data (Mandantendatenübernahme) agreed?

### 3.6 Required documents

| # | Document | Required for | Notes |
|---|----------|--------------|-------|
| D1 | Articles of association (Gesellschaftsvertrag / notarielle Urkunde) | NG-01, NG-02 | PDF scan is sufficient |
| D2 | Commercial register extract (Handelsregisterauszug) | NG-01, NG-02 | Current extract; if entry pending, the notary's filing confirmation |
| D3 | Shareholder list (Gesellschafterliste) | NG-01, NG-02 | |
| D4 | Proof of share capital payment (bank statement) | NG-01 | |
| D5 | Trade registration (Gewerbeanmeldung) | NG-01 | If the business is a Gewerbe |
| D6 | ID copies of managing directors | NG-01, NG-02 | For our anti-money-laundering (GwG) identification |
| D7 | Transparency register confirmation | NG-01, NG-02 | Or confirmation that registration is pending |
| D8 | Employment contracts | PR | One per employee |
| D9 | Employee master data sheet (Personalfragebogen) | PR | Our template, one per employee |
| D10 | Tax ID and social security number per employee | PR | Usually part of D9 |
| D11 | Signed engagement letter and power of attorney (Vollmacht) | all | Sent by us after intake – do not request from the client before the adviser has reviewed the case |

## 4. Standard follow-up questions

Ask only what is still missing. Do not ask the client for information they already gave. Group questions: no more than three per message.

Priority order for NG-01 with BK + PR:
1. Company name, registered office, exact founding date, Handelsregister status
2. Tax registration: Fragebogen submitted? Tax number?
3. Employees: count, start dates, employment type, salaries, managing director status
4. Bookkeeping: software, bank account, document flow
5. Previous adviser

## 5. Escalation rules (always to a human adviser)

Escalate and flag the case **high priority** if any of the following applies:

- The Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung has not been submitted and the company was founded more than three weeks ago. (Internal rule: we want to submit well within the statutory deadline – advisers check the deadline.)
- First payroll run is due within 30 days and no Betriebsnummer exists.
- A managing director is also a shareholder (social-security status assessment needed).
- Cross-border elements: foreign shareholders, foreign permanent establishment, employees working abroad.
- The client asks for a binding tax assessment, tax optimisation advice, or a statement on a specific tax liability.
- VAT questions beyond "please register us" (e.g. Kleinunternehmer, reverse charge, Organschaft).
- Any indication of missed deadlines, penalties, or correspondence from the Finanzamt.

The intake assistant never answers these substantively. It acknowledges, notes the issue in the case file and says an adviser will follow up.

## 6. Priority

| Priority | Criteria |
|----------|----------|
| Urgent | Deadline within 7 days, or Finanzamt letter with deadline |
| High | Any escalation rule in section 5 applies |
| Normal | Complete new-client intake, no deadline pressure |
| Low | Informational request, no mandate yet |

## 7. Handover to the adviser

The intake summary must contain: category and tags, master data, tax registration status, documents received, documents missing, open questions, escalation flags with reasons, recommended next action, and the sources used. The adviser, not the intake team, sends the engagement letter (D11).

## 8. Client communication style

- Address the client formally ("Sie") in German, or in the language they write in.
- Short paragraphs, numbered document lists.
- Never promise a deadline on behalf of the Finanzamt.
- Always sign as "Ihr Team von Muster & Partner Steuerberatung".
