# DATEV – how data typically flows between a client and a tax firm

> **STARTER NOTE.** Summary prepared for the TaxHub demo from DATEV's public product descriptions. Replace with official DATEV help-center articles (https://www.datev.de/hilfe) or product sheets. Product names and features change; do not rely on this note for configuration details.

## Where TaxHub sits

TaxHub is an intake and workflow layer **before** DATEV. Once a case is accepted, the tax firm creates the client (Mandant) in its DATEV environment and processes bookkeeping and payroll there.

## Relevant DATEV products (public descriptions)

- **DATEV Unternehmen online** – a cloud platform where the client uploads receipts and bank data and the tax firm processes them. Common default for firms that do the client's bookkeeping.
- **DATEV Kanzlei-Rechnungswesen** – the tax firm's bookkeeping and year-end software.
- **DATEV Lohn und Gehalt / DATEV LODAS** – payroll programs used by tax firms to run monthly payroll, including wage-tax and social-security reporting.

## Clients using third-party bookkeeping software (e.g. Lexoffice)

Many small-business tools, including Lexoffice, offer a **DATEV export** (bookings in the DATEV format) so the tax firm can import the client's data into its DATEV bookkeeping. Before accepting a client who uses such software, the firm should clarify:

- who does the bookkeeping (client in the tool, or the firm in DATEV)
- whether the export or an interface is set up
- how receipts reach the firm
