# DATEV – Digital mandate initiation and client communication

## Source status

This knowledge note is based on public DATEV documentation describing how tax advisory firms handle prospective-client enquiries, onboarding, communication, tasks and document exchange.

DATEV already provides substantial digital functionality for these workflows. TaxHub is therefore positioned as an AI interaction layer around existing DATEV processes rather than as a replacement for DATEV.

## Incoming enquiries

DATEV states that prospective clients can contact a tax advisory firm through several channels, including:

- telephone
- email
- personal contacts
- DATEV SmartExperts

DATEV notes that reviewing incoming enquiries, consolidating information and entering the relevant data has historically required significant time and manual effort.

Enquiries originating through DATEV SmartExperts can be transferred directly into DATEV's prospect-management system.

Where an enquiry arrives through channels such as email, telephone or a personal contact, a new prospect can be created manually in the DATEV prospect-management system.

## Prospect onboarding

DATEV provides a digital onboarding workflow for prospective clients.

Prospects can be invited to enter required data themselves and securely send that information to the tax advisory firm.

Tax firms can also create and exchange questionnaires with prospective clients.

Once the prospect submits the requested data, the tax firm receives a notification and the information becomes available in the DATEV prospect-management environment for further processing.

Selected data and documents can also be transferred into DATEV DMS.

## Digital client communication

MyDATEV Kanzlei provides a secure digital workspace for communication between tax firms and their clients.

DATEV describes this as a way to centralise communication around:

- documents
- questions
- tasks
- approvals
- conversations

The stated aim is to reduce fragmented communication across channels such as email and WhatsApp and to create a central digital workspace for matters requiring coordination between the firm and the client.

## Document exchange

Tax firms can securely provide documents to clients through MyDATEV and receive documents from clients.

DATEV also provides search and filter functionality within the available document set.

Documents can be exchanged together with messages where further explanation or context is required.

## Tasks and follow-up

Tax firms can create tasks for clients, for example when documents still need to be supplied or reviewed.

Tasks can include information such as:

- due date
- responsibility
- requested documents or information

DATEV also supports communication around tasks when additional questions arise.

## Repeated communication

DATEV provides reusable text modules for common communication situations, including:

- appointment coordination
- follow-up questions
- requests for information

These can be customised by the tax advisory firm.

This indicates that repeated communication and information-request workflows are a meaningful part of day-to-day tax-firm operations.

## Relevance to TaxHub

DATEV already provides strong structured workflows once a client or prospect is inside the DATEV environment.

TaxHub focuses on the unstructured interaction layer before and around those workflows.

Examples include:

- understanding a natural-language client message
- identifying the type of request
- extracting information already provided
- identifying missing information
- asking targeted follow-up questions
- determining which documents are still required
- producing a structured case summary
- drafting a client response
- preparing information for transfer into the firm's existing DATEV workflow

TaxHub should therefore be positioned as complementary to DATEV rather than competitive with it.

## Example TaxHub workflow

Client message:

“I founded a GmbH three weeks ago, use Lexoffice, have two employees starting next month and need help with bookkeeping and payroll.”

TaxHub can:

1. classify the enquiry
2. extract the information already supplied
3. identify missing information
4. ask targeted follow-up questions
5. request relevant supporting documents
6. produce a structured adviser-ready case
7. prepare the next communication for the client

The resulting structured information could later be transferred into the firm's DATEV environment.

## Product boundary

TaxHub does not replace:

- DATEV accounting applications
- DATEV tax applications
- DATEV DMS
- DATEV mandate-management systems
- MyDATEV Kanzlei

Its intended role is to reduce the manual work involved in translating unstructured client communication into structured, actionable information.
