# ELSTER – Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung for a Kapitalgesellschaft (GmbH)

> **STARTER NOTE.** Summary prepared for the TaxHub demo from the publicly described structure of the ELSTER form. Replace with the official ELSTER help pages or a PDF export of the form (see /knowledge/README.md) before relying on it. Field names and sections can change between form versions.

## Where to find it

In "Mein ELSTER" the form is listed under the formation forms (Formulare & Leistungen → Alle Formulare → Unternehmensgründung) as **"Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung – Gründung einer Kapitalgesellschaft / Genossenschaft"**. Submission requires an ELSTER account (organisation certificate) or can be done by a tax adviser on the company's behalf.

## Legal basis

The electronic transmission is mandatory under § 138 Abs. 1b AO (see separate note). The questionnaire must be submitted within one month (§ 138 Abs. 4 AO / § 137 Abs. 2 AO).

## Typical information requested

The questionnaire for a Kapitalgesellschaft typically asks for:

1. **General company data** – name, legal form, registered office and address, contact details, business purpose, Handelsregister entry (court and number, or date of application), date of articles of association.
2. **Shareholders and managing directors** – names, addresses, tax identification numbers, shareholdings.
3. **Tax adviser** – whether a tax adviser is appointed and whether they are authorised to receive correspondence.
4. **Bank details** – business account for refunds and direct debit.
5. **Details on profit determination** – financial year, start of business activity, expected profits for the founding year and the following year.
6. **Turnover tax (Umsatzsteuer)** – expected turnover for the founding year and following year, request for a VAT ID (USt-IdNr.), taxation method.
7. **Wage tax (Lohnsteuer)** – whether employees are employed, number of employees (including Minijobs), expected wage tax, start of payroll.

## Result

After processing, the Finanzamt assigns a tax number (Steuernummer) and, if requested, the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern issues a VAT ID.
