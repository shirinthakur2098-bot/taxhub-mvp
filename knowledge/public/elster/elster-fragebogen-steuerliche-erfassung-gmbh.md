# ELSTER – Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung for a Kapitalgesellschaft (GmbH)

## Source status

This file is based on the official ELSTER help page for the
“Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung bei Gründung einer Kapitalgesellschaft / Genossenschaft”.

A GmbH is explicitly listed by ELSTER as an eligible type of corporation for this questionnaire.

## Core filing requirement

When a business is opened, the responsible Finanzamt must generally be informed within one month.

For a newly founded Kapitalgesellschaft such as a GmbH, this is done using the
“Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung: Gründung einer Kapitalgesellschaft bzw. Genossenschaft”.

The questionnaire must generally be transmitted electronically through the officially designated interface, for example through Mein ELSTER.

Paper submission is only permitted in exceptional hardship cases.

Legal basis referenced by ELSTER:
- § 138 Abs. 1b AO
- § 138 Abs. 4 AO

ELSTER also notes that certain changes relevant for tax registration, including acquisition of legal capacity, changes of legal form, relocation of management or registered office, and dissolution, must be reported to the responsible Finanzamt within one month after the relevant event.

## Information relevant to a new GmbH intake

The ELSTER guidance indicates that the tax registration process can require information relating to areas such as:

- company identity and registered office
- nature and scope of the business activity
- authorised recipient / tax adviser
- bank details
- financial year
- employment and payroll
- expected wage tax
- expected turnover
- VAT treatment
- VAT identification number where relevant

TaxHub should not assume that every field applies to every client. It should ask only the follow-up questions relevant to the client’s situation.

## Payroll-related information

Where the company has employees, ELSTER asks for the number of employees and separately identifies certain categories such as marginally employed staff.

The questionnaire also requests information about expected wage tax where applicable.

ELSTER states that electronic wage-tax registrations are due by the end of the 10th day following the relevant filing period.

## VAT-related information

The ELSTER guidance includes questions about expected turnover and VAT treatment.

Where VAT advance returns are required, ELSTER states that they are generally transmitted electronically by the 10th day after the relevant filing period.

The form also contains information relating to VAT identification numbers where applicable.

## Bank details

The questionnaire includes bank-account information.

For foreign bank accounts, additional information such as the BIC may be required.
