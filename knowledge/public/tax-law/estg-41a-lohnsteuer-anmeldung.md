# Einkommensteuergesetz § 41a – Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung

> **STARTER NOTE.** Paraphrased summary prepared for the TaxHub demo. Verify against the official text at https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__41a.html and replace with the official document before production use.

## Obligation

An employer must submit a **Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung** to the Betriebsstättenfinanzamt no later than the **10th day after the end of each Lohnsteuer-Anmeldungszeitraum**, and pay the withheld wage tax by the same date. The Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung is transmitted electronically via the official interface.

## Anmeldungszeitraum (reporting period)

The reporting period depends on the wage tax of the previous calendar year:

- **monthly**, if the wage tax to be withheld for the previous calendar year was more than 5,000 euros
- **quarterly**, if it was more than 1,080 euros but not more than 5,000 euros
- **annually**, if it was not more than 1,080 euros

If the business did not exist for the whole previous calendar year, the wage tax is annualised. If the business was only founded in the current year, the projected wage tax for the first full month, annualised, is used.

## Related employer obligations (outside § 41a)

- Employers retrieve employees' wage-tax deduction features electronically (ELStAM, § 39e EStG).
- Social-security registration of employees is governed by the SGB IV (see separate note), not by the EStG.

## Practical relevance for a new GmbH with employees

A GmbH that hires its first employees will need to submit Lohnsteuer-Anmeldungen for the relevant periods. The Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung asks for information about planned employees, which the Finanzamt uses to set up the wage-tax account. The exact period and amounts are for the adviser to determine.
