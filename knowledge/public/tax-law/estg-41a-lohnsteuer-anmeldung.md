# Einkommensteuergesetz § 41a – Anmeldung und Abführung der Lohnsteuer

## Source status

This knowledge note is based on the official text of § 41a of the German Income Tax Act (Einkommensteuergesetz, EStG), published through Gesetze im Internet.

It is used by TaxHub to support intake and case preparation where a GmbH employs staff and payroll obligations may be relevant.

## Filing and payment deadline

Under § 41a Abs. 1 EStG, an employer must submit a Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung to the responsible Betriebsstättenfinanzamt no later than the 10th day after the end of the relevant Lohnsteuer-Anmeldungszeitraum.

The employer must also remit the wage tax withheld during that reporting period by the same deadline.

## Electronic transmission

The Lohnsteuer-Anmeldung must generally be transmitted electronically using the officially prescribed data set and interface.

The Finanzamt may allow an exception from electronic transmission in cases of undue hardship.

## Reporting period

Under § 41a Abs. 2 EStG, the Lohnsteuer-Anmeldungszeitraum is generally the calendar month.

The reporting period is:

- monthly as the default
- quarterly where the wage tax payable for the previous calendar year was more than EUR 1,080 but not more than EUR 5,000
- annually where the wage tax payable for the previous calendar year was not more than EUR 1,080

If the business did not exist for the full previous calendar year, the relevant amount is annualised.

If the business did not yet exist in the previous calendar year, the annualised wage tax for the first full calendar month after opening the business is used to determine the reporting period.

## Relevance to a newly founded GmbH

Where a newly founded GmbH hires employees, TaxHub should identify payroll as a relevant workstream and capture information such as:

- whether employees have already started
- planned payroll start date
- number of employees
- whether payroll has already been set up
- whether Lohnsteuer-Anmeldungen have already been submitted
- whether the company already has a tax adviser handling payroll

TaxHub should not independently determine the final filing frequency or amount of wage tax due where professional judgment or additional facts are required.

## Example TaxHub scenario

Client message:

“We founded our GmbH three weeks ago and have two employees starting next month.”

TaxHub should:

1. recognise payroll as a relevant service requirement,
2. ask for the planned employment start date,
3. capture the number of employees,
4. determine whether payroll registration has already been started,
5. add payroll setup to the adviser-ready case,
6. flag the matter for professional review where necessary.

## Guardrail

TaxHub uses § 41a EStG to support intake and workflow preparation only.

It should not calculate wage tax, determine the client's final filing frequency without sufficient information, or provide unsupported tax advice.

Any such determination should be escalated to a qualified tax adviser.
