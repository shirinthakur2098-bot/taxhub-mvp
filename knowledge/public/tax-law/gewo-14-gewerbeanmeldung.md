# Gewerbeordnung § 14 – Anzeigepflicht

> **STARTER NOTE.** Paraphrased summary prepared for the TaxHub demo. Verify against https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__14.html.

Anyone who starts operating a standing trade (stehendes Gewerbe) must notify the responsible authority (usually the local Gewerbeamt) at the same time. This also applies to a GmbH that operates a trade.

The trade office forwards information from the Gewerbeanmeldung to other authorities, including the Finanzamt. This forwarding does **not** replace the separate obligation to submit the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung (see § 138 AO note).
