# Gewerbeordnung § 14 – Anzeigepflicht

## Source status

This knowledge note is based on the official text of § 14 of the German Trade Regulation Act (Gewerbeordnung, GewO), published through Gesetze im Internet.

It is used by TaxHub to support intake and case preparation where a client has recently started operating a commercial business.

## Starting a trade

Under § 14 Abs. 1 GewO, anyone who begins operating a standing trade (stehendes Gewerbe), a branch office or a dependent branch must notify the competent authority at the same time.

The notification obligation also applies where:

- the business is relocated
- the nature of the business changes
- the business activity is expanded to goods or services that are not customary for the registered type of business
- the trader's name changes
- the business is discontinued

For a GmbH carrying on a commercial activity, the Gewerbeanmeldung is therefore a relevant part of the formation and operating process.

## Information sharing with other authorities

Under § 14 Abs. 8 GewO, the competent trade authority regularly transmits data from the Gewerbeanzeige to a number of public bodies.

These include, among others:

- the Industrie- und Handelskammer
- the Handwerkskammer where relevant
- the Bundesagentur für Arbeit
- the responsible Berufsgenossenschaft through the Deutsche Gesetzliche Unfallversicherung
- certain state authorities
- the competent Finanzamt

The law expressly states that transmission to the Finanzamt is without prejudice to § 138 AO.

This means that the Gewerbeanmeldung does not replace the separate tax-registration obligations under the Abgabenordnung.

## Relevance to TaxHub

If a newly founded GmbH tells TaxHub that it has already registered the trade, TaxHub should not assume that tax registration has also been completed.

TaxHub should separately ask:

- Has the Gewerbeanmeldung been completed?
- When was the business registered?
- Has the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung also been submitted?
- Has the company received a Steuernummer?
- Has the company begun business activity?
- Is there any change of registered office, business activity or legal form that may require further notification?

## Example TaxHub scenario

Client message:

“We registered the company with the Gewerbeamt, so I assumed the Finanzamt already has everything.”

TaxHub should:

1. record that the Gewerbeanmeldung has been completed,
2. explain that data may be transmitted by the Gewerbeamt to the Finanzamt,
3. not treat that transmission as a substitute for the separate tax-registration process,
4. ask whether the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung has been submitted,
5. flag the issue for adviser review where necessary.

## Guardrail

TaxHub uses § 14 GewO to support intake and workflow preparation only.

It should not independently determine whether a particular activity qualifies as a Gewerbe, whether an exemption applies, or whether the client has fully satisfied all registration obligations.

Any such determination should be escalated to a qualified adviser.
