# Buchführungspflicht der GmbH

> **STARTER NOTE.** Paraphrased summary prepared for the TaxHub demo. Verify against the official texts (HGB § 238: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hgb/__238.html, GmbHG § 13: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__13.html, GmbHG § 41: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gmbhg/__41.html).

## GmbH as a merchant

- § 13 Abs. 3 GmbHG: A GmbH is deemed a trading company (Handelsgesellschaft) within the meaning of the HGB.
- § 6 Abs. 1 HGB: Provisions for merchants (Kaufleute) apply to trading companies.

## Obligation to keep books

- § 238 Abs. 1 HGB: Every merchant is obliged to keep books and to record in them his commercial transactions and the state of his assets in accordance with the principles of proper accounting (Grundsätze ordnungsmäßiger Buchführung).
- § 41 GmbHG: The managing directors are obliged to ensure proper bookkeeping of the company.

## Practical relevance

A GmbH must keep double-entry books from its founding. It is not eligible for the simplified cash-basis accounting (EÜR) available to many sole traders and freelancers. Whether the client or the adviser performs the bookkeeping is an organisational choice, not a legal one.
