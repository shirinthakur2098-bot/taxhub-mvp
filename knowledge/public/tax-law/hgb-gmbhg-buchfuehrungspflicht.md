# Buchführungspflicht der GmbH

## Source status

This knowledge note is based on the official texts of:

- § 238 HGB
- § 6 HGB
- § 13 GmbHG
- § 41 GmbHG

published through Gesetze im Internet.

It is used by TaxHub to identify bookkeeping as a relevant workstream when a GmbH approaches a tax advisory firm.

## GmbH as a commercial company

Under § 13 Abs. 3 GmbHG, a GmbH is treated as a Handelsgesellschaft within the meaning of the Handelsgesetzbuch.

Under § 6 Abs. 1 HGB, the provisions applying to merchants also apply to Handelsgesellschaften.

This is relevant because the HGB bookkeeping rules therefore apply to a GmbH.

## Obligation to keep books

Under § 238 Abs. 1 HGB, every merchant is required to keep books and record commercial transactions and the state of the company’s assets in accordance with the principles of proper accounting.

The bookkeeping must be organised so that a knowledgeable third party can obtain an overview of the business transactions and financial position within a reasonable period of time.

The origin and processing of transactions must also be traceable.

## Responsibility of the managing directors

Under § 41 GmbHG, the managing directors are responsible for ensuring that the company maintains proper bookkeeping.

For TaxHub, this means that bookkeeping is a relevant onboarding topic when a newly founded GmbH approaches a tax advisory firm.

## Relevance to a newly founded GmbH

If a client says:

“We founded a GmbH and use Lexoffice, but we are not sure how bookkeeping should be handled,”

TaxHub should:

1. identify bookkeeping as a relevant service requirement,
2. capture which accounting or invoicing software is currently used,
3. ask whether bookkeeping has already started,
4. determine whether a tax adviser or internal employee is currently responsible,
5. identify whether historical documents or opening records need to be provided,
6. prepare the case for professional review.

TaxHub should not independently determine the accounting treatment of individual transactions.

## Example intake questions

TaxHub may ask:

- Which bookkeeping software do you currently use?
- Has bookkeeping already started?
- Who is currently responsible for the bookkeeping?
- From which date are accounting records available?
- Are bank statements and invoices available digitally?
- Do you already have opening-balance or formation documents?
- Are there transactions from before the current bookkeeping setup that still need to be recorded?

## Guardrail

TaxHub uses these provisions to identify the existence of bookkeeping obligations and to prepare the client intake.

The system should not independently:

- determine the correct accounting treatment of individual transactions,
- prepare final financial statements,
- decide whether a particular accounting method is legally sufficient,
- provide unsupported accounting or tax conclusions.

These matters should be handled by a qualified tax adviser or other appropriately qualified professional.
