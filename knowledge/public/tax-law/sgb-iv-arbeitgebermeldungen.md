# SGB IV – Employer registration obligations

> **STARTER NOTE.** Paraphrased summary prepared for the TaxHub demo. Verify against the official text of § 28a SGB IV (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_4/__28a.html) and § 18i SGB IV (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_4/__18i.html).

## Betriebsnummer

An employer needs a **Betriebsnummer** before registering employees for social security. It is issued by the Betriebsnummern-Service of the Bundesagentur für Arbeit (§ 18i SGB IV).

## Registration of employees

§ 28a SGB IV: The employer must report each employee subject to social insurance to the responsible collecting agency (Einzugsstelle, i.e. the employee's statutory health insurer). Reports are made electronically, for example at the start of employment.

## Practical relevance

A new GmbH that will employ staff next month should request its Betriebsnummer early, because payroll registration depends on it. Whether a managing shareholder is subject to social insurance is a separate status assessment that must be done by an adviser.
