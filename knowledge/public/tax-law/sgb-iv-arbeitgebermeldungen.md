# SGB IV – Employer registration obligations

## Source status

This knowledge note is based on the official text of the German Social Code Book IV (SGB IV), in particular:

- § 28a SGB IV – Meldepflicht
- § 18i SGB IV – Betriebsnummer

It is used by TaxHub to support intake and case preparation where a newly founded GmbH plans to employ staff.

## Betriebsnummer

An employer needs a Betriebsnummer for social-security reporting.

Under § 18i SGB IV, the Betriebsnummer is assigned through the Bundesagentur für Arbeit.

For a newly founded GmbH that will employ staff, the Betriebsnummer is therefore a relevant onboarding item before payroll and social-security reporting can be handled correctly.

## Registration of employees

Under § 28a SGB IV, employers are subject to reporting obligations for employees who are covered by the statutory social-security system.

These reports are transmitted electronically to the responsible collecting agency.

Relevant reporting events can include, among other things:

- start of employment
- end of employment
- changes that affect social-security reporting
- annual or other required reports

The exact reporting obligation depends on the employee’s circumstances.

## Relevance to a newly founded GmbH

If a client says:

“We have two employees starting next month,”

TaxHub should identify payroll and social-security setup as relevant and ask whether the employer-side registrations are already in place.

Relevant intake questions may include:

- Has a Betriebsnummer already been issued?
- When do the employees start?
- How many employees will there be?
- Are the employees already registered with their health insurance funds?
- Who is responsible for payroll processing?
- Has payroll software or a payroll provider already been set up?
- Are any managing shareholders also employed by the company?

## Managing shareholders

Whether a managing shareholder is subject to statutory social insurance can depend on the specific ownership and control structure.

TaxHub should not determine this status independently.

If the case involves a managing shareholder or another potentially borderline employment relationship, it should be flagged for adviser review.

## Example TaxHub scenario

Client message:

“We founded our GmbH recently and have two employees starting next month.”

TaxHub should:

1. identify payroll and social-security setup as relevant,
2. ask whether a Betriebsnummer already exists,
3. capture the employees’ planned start dates,
4. identify whether payroll and social-security registration have already been arranged,
5. include any missing setup steps in the adviser-ready case,
6. escalate questions requiring a status assessment or professional judgment.

## Guardrail

TaxHub uses the SGB IV material only to support intake and workflow preparation.

It should not independently determine:

- whether a specific person is subject to social insurance,
- the correct contribution treatment,
- whether a status determination procedure is required,
- the final content or timing of a specific social-security filing.

These matters should be handled by a qualified adviser or payroll professional.
